## Error Detection MCQs with Solutions

---

### 1.

Somebody has **(A)** / broken the window **(B)** / by throwing **(C)** / many stone at it. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

#### Options:

* A
* B
* C
* D
* E

### Answer: **D**

### Solution:

“many stone” is incorrect because after **many**, we use a **plural noun**.

✅ Correct sentence:
**“many stones at it.”**

---

* A

### Solution:

“child” should be plural because “they” refers to multiple children.

# Error Detection MCQs (40 Questions with Solutions)

---

# 1.

Each of the boys **(A)** / were given **(B)** / a certificate for **(C)** / participation. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

### Answer: **B**

### Solution:

“Each” is singular, so singular verb should be used.

✅ Correct: **was given**

---

# 2.

Neither the teacher nor the students **(A)** / was aware **(B)** / of the changes made **(C)** / in the schedule. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

### Answer: **B**

### Solution:

When subjects are joined by “neither…nor”, the verb agrees with the nearer subject (“students”).

✅ Correct: **were aware**

---

# 3.

One of my friend **(A)** / has started **(B)** / a new business **(C)** / in Delhi. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

### Answer: **A**

### Solution:

After “one of”, noun should be plural.

✅ Correct: **friends**

---

# 4.

She is senior than **(A)** / me in experience **(B)** / but junior in **(C)** / age. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

### Answer: **A**

### Solution:

With “senior”, “junior”, “superior”, we use **to**, not **than**.

✅ Correct: **senior to me**

---

# 5.

The committee have **(A)** / submitted its report **(B)** / to the chairman **(C)** / yesterday. **(D)** / No error **(E)**

### Answer: **A**

### Solution:

“Committee” is singular collective noun here.

✅ Correct: **has submitted**

# 6.

“Deny” is followed by gerund (verb + ing).

# 7.

# 11.

* A
* B
* C
* D
* E

### Answer: **A**

### Solution:

The sentence needs “what” instead of “how people do”.

✅ Correct sentence:
**“What people do to earn their bread…”**

* B

* E

### Solution:

**“projects you are handling”**
